# Upgrade, outage and recovery watch

Use during an announced service change or an actively followed recovery. Keep
brand-specific dates, products and case IDs in the project's dated watch plan.
The plan is a research aid, not permission to start a recurring monitor.

## Compare the correct facts

Refresh official notices and revisions during a live pass. Record the notice URL,
observation time, published timezone, product/asset/operation scope and any
visible revision. A failed refresh means stale context, not unchanged facts.

Separate planned deadlines, estimated restoration and observed restoration.
An elapsed estimate triggers a check; it proves neither an outage nor recovery.
Compare each complaint's own claimed event time and product against the notice.
Missing scope stays unknown. A later notice does not prove earlier warning;
coincident timing does not establish causation.

A question about a disclosed pause is not automatically an incident. Conversely,
an early, out-of-scope or persistent restriction, alleged loss or unexpected order
closure requires separate case review even when maintenance was announced.
An operational status table establishes only its stated scope, not every login,
balance, API function or user account. Do not test trading, withdrawals or account
actions as part of passive monitoring.

## Reserve priority reads

Within the existing run deadline, prioritize:

1. Current official threads and relevant public replies/quotes, including replies
   without the brand keyword. Verify account identity before attributing support.
2. Original links and follow-up for active unresolved cases, even when their
   origin is older than the discovery window.
3. Short searches across actual impact families: access/funds, order handling,
   identity/history, feature recovery and migration impersonation.
4. Independent forums, reviews, news and separate post/comment search surfaces.

Sample distinct risk families and languages seen in relevant complaints. Avoid
the Cartesian product of every synonym, language and platform. Record any
priority read omitted because of access or the deadline.

Inspect claim-critical media when available. Support text quoted by a complainant
is not an authenticated admission. Observe alleged migration phishing through
public evidence without opening suspicious destinations, connecting wallets or
contacting the accounts.

## Preserve case state

Record actual follow-up separately from material developments. A general recovery
announcement cannot close an individual case. Attribute case-specific outcomes as
user-confirmed, support-claimed, independently observed or disputed/unknown.
Do not auto-close incidents when the plan expires, and do not keep an expired
upgrade watch as a permanent source of “new” incidents. Review relevance while
retaining unresolved cases and their remaining evidence needs.

If the dated plan is absent, name the missing scope. Ordinary monitoring may
continue within its authorized scope; an upgrade-specific report must not claim
that it covered an invented plan. One bounded pass is not continuous surveillance.
